This site is the container for article writing, notes, demos, collections, and
any other public artifacts that need a home.

## Possible shelves

- `/articles/` for blog-style writing.
- `/site-notes/` for notes about the site itself.
- `/tools/` for small utilities or demos.

The goal is not to predefine every category. The goal is to make adding the next
thing feel cheap.
